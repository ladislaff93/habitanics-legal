Frequently asked questions

What is Habitanics?

Habitanics is a habit tracker for iOS that turns your daily habits into a farming game. Every habit you create plants a crop in your garden. Completing the habit keeps the crop healthy and growing; skipping it makes the crop wilt. When a crop is fully grown you harvest it for coins, which you can trade at the market for new seeds.

Is Habitanics free?

Habitanics is free to download and starts with a free trial so you can try everything. After the trial, a premium subscription is required to keep growing your garden.

What happens if I miss a habit?

Your crop's health drops and it starts to wilt. Crop health recovers as you get back on track, so one bad day won't kill a thriving garden — but a week of skipped habits will. The garden is an honest mirror of your consistency.

Is Habitanics good for ADHD?

Many gamified habit trackers work well for ADHD brains because they replace abstract checklists with immediate, visual feedback. In Habitanics you see your progress as a living garden, get instant rewards (coins, XP, growth), and the gentle loss mechanic (wilting crops) creates just enough urgency without punishing streak resets.

How is Habitanics different from Habitica or Finch?

Habitica is an RPG with parties and quests, and Finch is a self-care pet. Habitanics is a cozy farming game: each habit is its own crop with its own health, so you can see exactly which habits are thriving and which are dying at a glance. It focuses on a calm, visual economy — grow, harvest, trade, expand. Read our full comparison of gamified habit trackers.

Is Habitanics available on Android?

Not yet. Habitanics is currently iOS only, built natively with the latest SwiftUI. An Android version may come later.
