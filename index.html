Frequently asked questions

What is Habitanics?

Habitanics is a habit tracker for iOS that turns your daily habits into a farming game. Every habit you create plants a crop in your garden. Completing the habit keeps the crop healthy and growing; skipping it makes the crop wilt. When a crop is fully grown you harvest it for coins, which you can trade at the market for new seeds. Learn more →

Is Habitanics free?

Habitanics is free to download and starts with a free trial so you can try everything. After the trial, a premium subscription is required to keep growing your garden. Learn more →

What happens if I miss a habit?

Your crop's health drops and it starts to wilt. Crop health recovers as you get back on track, so one bad day won't kill a thriving garden — but a week of skipped habits will. The garden is an honest mirror of your consistency, with no streak resets. Learn more →

Is Habitanics good for ADHD?

Many gamified habit trackers work well for ADHD brains because they replace abstract checklists with immediate, visual feedback. In Habitanics you see your progress as a living garden, get instant rewards (coins, XP, growth), and the gentle loss mechanic (wilting crops) creates just enough urgency without punishing streak resets. Learn more →

How do rewards work in Habitanics?

Every completed habit earns XP instantly, and fully grown crops are harvested for coins. Harder habits grow more valuable crops, coins buy new seeds at the market, and XP levels you up to unlock more garden tiles — a full reward economy, not just points. Learn more →

Can Habitanics help me build a daily routine?

Yes — plant a crop for each keystone habit, set honest difficulties, add reminders, and check your garden once a day. A wilting crop tells you which habit needs shrinking or re-anchoring long before the routine collapses. Learn more →

How long does it take to build a habit?

Research puts habit formation at about 66 days on average, ranging from 18 to 254 days depending on the habit and the person. What matters most is total repetitions — missing a single day has almost no measurable effect. Learn more →

How is Habitanics different from Habitica or Finch?

Habitica is an RPG with parties and quests, and Finch is a self-care pet. Habitanics is a cozy farming game: each habit is its own crop with its own health, so you can see exactly which habits are thriving and which are dying at a glance. It focuses on a calm, visual economy — grow, harvest, trade, expand. Learn more →

Is Habitanics available on Android?

Not yet. Habitanics is currently iOS only, built natively with the latest SwiftUI. An Android version may come later. Meanwhile, you can use our free printable habit tracker on any device. Learn more →
