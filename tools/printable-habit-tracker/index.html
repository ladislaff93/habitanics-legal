How to use a habit tracker (so it actually works)

Put the printed grid somewhere you can't avoid — the fridge, your desk, inside your planner. Mark each habit immediately after doing it; the small act of crossing a box is a real reward signal. Keep the list short: 3–5 habits beats 15. And when you miss a day, just don't miss two — research shows a single missed day doesn't hurt habit formation, but breaking the chain twice in a row often becomes quitting.
